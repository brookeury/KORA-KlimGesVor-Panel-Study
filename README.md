# KORA-KlimGesVor-Panel-Study

This repository contains coding files to clean and prepare data from the KORA (Cooperative Health Research in the Region of Augsburg) climate panel study (KlimGesVor). This panel study contains data from 12 monthly observations of 123 individuals living with chronic illness (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, hypertension, and type 2 diabetes) in Augsburg, Germany. Health outcomes are matched to meterological and air pollution variables to determine environmental exposures.

This data is not publicly available, but may be requested from the KORA.PASST webite: https://helmholtz-muenchen.managed-otrs.com/external/

For more details of a use case for this data, please refer to the following poster presentation from the International Society for Environmental Epidemiology conference 2026 that explored the impact of temperature on lung function outcomes within the KORA KlimGesVor Panel Study:
B Ury, R Pickford, K Wolf, A Peters, B Linkohr, S Karrasch, M Heier, H Bühnert, C Flexeder, S Breitner-Busch,  Schneider. Impact of Short-Term Temperature on Lung Function: Panel Study of a Population of Older Adults with Chronic Noncommunicable Diseases. (In preparation). doi: https://doi.org/10.82296/hmgu-nefeli.bexyw-d6k04
